3.2　Magnetic Drive Assembly

The magnetic drive transmits torque from the motor to the impeller shaft without a mechanical seal penetrating the pressure boundary. This eliminates the principal leakage path of a packed-gland autoclave.

Before each run, verify that the coupling bearing has not exceeded its service interval. Operating beyond 1,800 hours markedly increases the risk of crystalline deposits seizing the inner rotor.

3.2.1　Service Limits

ItemLimit
Coupling bearing1,800 h
Max stirring speed1,200 rpm
Jacket oil temp.300 ℃

Should the burst disc actuate, the entire disc assembly must be replaced; field repair is not permitted under any circumstances.

3.2　磁力传动组件

磁力传动将电机扭矩传递至搅拌轴，无需机械密封穿越压力边界，由此消除了填料密封式高压反应釜的主要泄漏通道。

每次运行前应确认耦合轴承未超出保养周期。运行超过 1,800 小时后，结晶沉积卡滞内磁转子的风险显著升高。

3.2.1　使用限值

项目限值
耦合轴承1,800 h
最高转速1,200 rpm
夹套导热油温度300 ℃

爆破片一旦动作，须整组更换；任何情况下均不允许现场修复。
